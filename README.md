# DittoClip

Clipboard history for macOS. DittoClip remembers the text, links and images you copy, so you can find and reuse them from any app.

## Download

**[Download DittoClip.dmg](https://github.com/DittoClip/DittoClip/releases/latest/download/DittoClip.dmg)** (latest version). Every version is on the [Releases page](https://github.com/DittoClip/DittoClip/releases).

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later.

## Install

1. Open `DittoClip.dmg` and drag **DittoClip** into your **Applications** folder.
2. Open DittoClip from Applications. If macOS says it can't verify the app, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
3. Press **Control-Shift-V**, or click 📋 in the menu bar, to open your clipboard history.
